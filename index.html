Latest News & Tax Tips

Stay current on the latest events

Individual Jul 4, 2025

One Big Beautiful Bill Act – Individual Tax Changes

+

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA), signed into law on July 4, 2025, introduces significant changes for individual taxpayers. The Act permanently extends current individual income tax brackets and higher standard deduction amounts, indexed annually for inflation.

Source: IRS
Business Jul 4, 2025

One Big Beautiful Bill Act – Business Tax Changes

+

The OBBBA provides long-term certainty for businesses by making several major provisions permanent. The Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction remains at 20%, and bonus depreciation returns to 100%.

Source: AICPA
International Jul 4, 2025

One Big Beautiful Bill Act – International & Energy Provisions

+

Internationally, the Act restructures the global minimum tax framework and establishes a permanent Opportunity Zone framework beginning in 2027.

Source: Holland & Knight

Preparation of Tax Returns & Tax Planning

+

We believe that the best way to maximize the tax benefits that are available for you or your business, effective tax planning is needed. Every client is different and may need different advice and time to help reach individual goals. We provide services to reach these goals.


We prepare many types of tax returns including:
  • Individual Returns
  • Partnership Returns
  • Corporate Returns (1120 & 1120S)
  • Non-Profit Returns (Form 990, 990-EZ & 990-PF)
  • Estate and Gift Returns
  • Preparation of Personal and Business Financial Statements
  • We also assist clients with IRS and State notices or audits if needed.

Business Consulting & Appraisals

Estate & Trust Planning

+

Estate planning is the process of anticipating and arranging for the disposal of an estate during your life.


Many of the items we participate in planning are:
  • Estate Devices
  • Tax Planning
  • Estate Planner (collecting all data)
  • Beneficiary planning

In additions to estate planning, we assist in estate administration and tax return preparation for the estate.
